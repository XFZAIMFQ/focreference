Formula sheet

Everything the site derives, in one place, with this site's conventions applied: amplitude-invariant scaling, per-phase parameters, d along the rotor flux.

The worked numbers beside them are computed from src/core when this page is built, so they are the same values the figures use rather than a copy that can drift.

Frames

Clarke, amplitude-invariant

iα​=32​(ia​−21​ib​−21​ic​),iβ​=3​1​(ib​−ic​)

The 2/3 keeps the peak. With a floating neutral the third current is redundant.

Park

id​=iα​cosθe​+iβ​sinθe​,iq​=−iα​sinθe​+iβ​cosθe​

A rotation, so it preserves magnitude and the dot product — and therefore power.

Power

p=23​(vd​id​+vq​iq​)

The 3/2 is the amplitude-invariant convention, not a fudge.

The machine

dq voltage equations

vd​=Rs​id​+Ld​dtdid​​−ωe​Lq​iq​vq​=Rs​iq​+Lq​dtdiq​​+ωe​(Ld​id​+λm​)​

The cross terms are what decoupling cancels; the λm​ term is the back-EMF.

Torque

Te​=23​p[λm​iq​+(Ld​−Lq​)id​iq​]

Magnet term plus reluctance term. On this machine Lq​>Ld​, so negative id​ adds torque. Torque constant 0.051 N·m/A at id​=0.

saliency Lq​/Ld​ = 1.57

Characteristic current

ich​=−Ld​λm​​

Decides the whole field-weakening envelope. A machine whose rated current reaches it can be weakened indefinitely.

70.8 A on the reference machine

Back-EMF constant

Ke​=2​3​​λm​ωe,1000​

Line-to-line RMS per 1000 rpm — the form a datasheet quotes. Three conversions stand between it and λm​.

The inverter

Linear voltage limit

∣v∣max​=3​Vdc​​

The inscribed circle of the hexagon. SPWM reaches only Vdc​/2 — 15.5% less.

27.7 V against 24 V at 48 V

SVPWM dwell times

t1​=Vdc​3​∣v∣​sin(3π​−γ),t2​=Vdc​3​∣v∣​sinγt0​=1−t1​−t2​​

γ is the angle within the sector. The switching order is decided by the switch words, not by which vector is trailing.

Deadtime voltage error

Δv=Ts​td​​Vdc​sgn(i)

Sign follows the current, so it is a square wave at the electrical frequency — 6× ripple in dq.

Bridge loss

P=3Irms2​Rds(on)​+3fpwm​Vdc​∣i∣​tsw​

Square-law loss takes the RMS; linear loss takes the mean of the absolute sine, 0.6366. A lower Rds(on)​ moves the crossover down.

Control

Current-loop gains

kp​=ωc​L,ki​=ωc​Rs​

Pole-zero cancellation leaves a pure integrator, so the closed loop is first order at ωc​.

kp​ = 1.319, ki​ = 2199 for 1 kHz on the d axis

Anti-windup tracking gain

kaw​=kp​ki​​=LRs​​

Back-calculation. Too low and the integrator still runs away, just slower.

Phase margin from delay

PM=90∘−ωc​Td​

The whole stability story of a well-tuned current loop. A timing property, not a gain property.

75 µs of delay gives 63° at 1 kHz

Angle prediction

θout​=θmeasured​+ωe​Td​

Removes a speed-dependent rotation entirely. Does nothing for phase margin.

Cascade separation

ωouter​ωinner​​≥5 to 10

10× costs nothing, 5× starts to cost, under 3× doubles overshoot.

Sensing

Low-side measurement window

twindow​=(1−d)Ts​

Closes as duty approaches 1. The phase with the largest duty fails first.

Converter resolution

q=2N2Ifs​​,σ=12​q​

In dq the RMS is 2/3​ of the per-phase figure, and the peak bound is 2/3 of a code — not a half.

Encoder speed step

Δω=CPR⋅T2π​

Independent of speed, so it grows as a fraction of the measurement as the machine slows.

Link capacitor current

Icap​=Irms2​−Imean2​​

Exact, not approximate — mean and ripple are orthogonal. Peaks near half modulation depth.
